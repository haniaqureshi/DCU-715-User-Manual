# Email Settings

import Figure from "@site/src/components/Figure";

Use the **Email Settings** function to enter or update the email address associated with the DCU.
To open the Enter Email modal, proceed as follows:
1. From the Settings submenu, select Email Settings. 
2. The Enter Email module will appear as shown in <FigureRef id="Enter-Email-Module" />.

## Enter Email module 
The following fields and controls available in the Enter Email popup screen.

| Field/Button | Description                                                                              |                                                                                                                                          
| :----------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Email        | Displays the email address entered by the technician. <br />**Example:** clymerj@itr.com |
| Ok           | Save the entered email address and return to the sub-menu screen.                        |
| Cancel       | Closes the Enter Email modal without saving changes.                                     |

## Enter the email address

To enter the email address, proceed as follows:

    1. From the Settings submenu, select Email Settings. The Enter Email module will appear.
    2. Select the Email field. The on-screen keyboard appears as shown in Figure 5.4.3.
    3. Use the on-screen keyboard to enter the email address.

    :::info

        <ul><li>Use the **?123** key to switch to numbers and special characters.</li>
        <li>Use the **@** key to insert the at symbol.</li>
        <li>Use the **Backspace** key to remove the last entered character.</li></ul>

    :::

    4. After adding the email select **Ok**. The DCU saves the email address and returns to the Settings submenu.
    5. To close the Enter Email modal without saving changes, select **Cancel**.
